También: Brasil pide bloquear 5.209 sitios de apuestas; La Corte libera la venta de tierras a extranjeros; Ambito: BCRA registra la menor compra mensual del año.

#### 1. Banco de la República sube la tasa a 12,25%

La Junta Directiva del Banco de la República subió por mayoría la tasa 25 puntos básicos, a 12,25%. Cuatro directores apoyaron el aumento, dos votaron por mantenerla y uno por subirla 50 puntos básicos. La inflación anual de agosto fue 6,2%; la básica alcanzó 6,1%, su máximo del último año.

**Por qué importa.** La inflación básica en su máximo del último año reduce el margen para pasar a una política menos restrictiva.

Brasil pidió bloquear 5.209 sitios de apuestas y más de 2.000 perfiles tras la prohibición de Lula del 25 de septiembre. Según Estadao, el Congreso tiene hasta cuatro meses para avalarla y clubes de fútbol la disputan ante el STF. El medio fija el retiro de saldos hasta las 23h59 del 5 de octubre.

### Banco de la República raises rate to 12.25%

Also: Brazil requests blocking of 5,209 betting sites; Supreme Court lifts limits on foreign land purchases; Ambito: BCRA records year’s lowest monthly dollar purchases.

#### 1. Banco de la República raises rate to 12.25%

The Junta Directiva of Banco de la República raised the rate by 25 basis points to 12.25% by majority vote. Four directors backed the increase, two voted to hold and one sought a 50-basis-point rise. Annual inflation in August was 6.2%; core inflation reached 6.1%, its highest in the past year.

**Why it matters.** Core inflation at its highest in the past year limits room to move toward less restrictive monetary policy.

#### 2. Brazil requests blocking of 5,209 betting sites

Brazil requested blocks on 5,209 betting sites and more than 2,000 profiles after Lula’s September 25 ban. According to Estadao, Congress has up to four months to endorse it, while football clubs challenge it before the STF. The outlet gives bettors until 23h59 on October 5 to withdraw balances.

**Why it matters.** Congressional approval and the court challenge could shape the ban’s duration and scope.

#### 3. Supreme Court lifts limits on foreign land purchases

The Supreme Court overturned the ruling blocking Milei’s 2023 DNU, which repealed the land law. According to Clarin, article 154 of DNU 70/2023 remains in force, and Axel Kicillof called the ruling an “extremely serious decision.” Ambito reports that opposition lawmakers want Diputados to discuss the decree again.

**Why it matters.** A review of the decree in Diputados would offer a political route to challenge the opening enabled by the ruling.

#### 4. Ambito: BCRA records year’s lowest monthly dollar purchases

According to Ambito, BCRA bought USD 160 million and accumulated USD 473 million in September, the year’s lowest monthly total. Gross reserves fell USD 1,392 million in the session to USD 46,090 million, the outlet reported.

**Why it matters.** Net dollar purchases alone do not measure reserve accumulation: assessing the position also requires tracking the gross reserve stock.
