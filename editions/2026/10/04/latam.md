#### 1. Estadao: Quaest puts Lula at 46%, Flávio Bolsonaro at 45%

El Universal reported that a former Suchiapa mayor was also arrested and that the two officials were detained alongside 19 other people, including Municipal Police commanders and officers. In an initial report, the outlet said the Chiapas prosecutor's office had not disclosed the alleged offences. A later report mentioned corruption and criminal association among them. The SSPC publication confirms the arrest total and the mayor's detention but does not detail the charges.

**Why it matters.** The reported involvement of officials and police makes this a municipal leadership issue, not just a case involving private individuals. Officially specified charges will be needed to assess responsibility without treating arrests as convictions.

#### 7. Clarin: Rio Tinto plans a third RIGI for 2027

According to Clarin, Rio Tinto plans to seek a third RIGI for 2027 and is asking for infrastructure improvements in Argentina. Jérôme Pécresse, chief executive for Aluminium and Lithium, said this during Argentina Week in París. The company already has 2 lithium deposits under RIGI and is assessing copper-related projects.

The articles describe an evaluation and a plan for a third RIGI, not an already approved new investment; they identify neither a project nor an amount.

**Why it matters.** A copper opportunity would broaden the described focus beyond lithium deposits. Assessing the plan's scope requires identifying the project that would seek entry into RIGI.

#### 8. Folha: court order removes Datafolha's Ceará governor poll

Folha de S.Paulo said it removed Datafolha's Ceará governor poll from its website under a court order issued on Saturday. The survey was registered with the TSE under CE-09234/2026 and BR-06469/2026. According to the newspaper, the reason was a formal registration issue, not problems with the survey.

Estadao had reported 50% of valid votes for Elmano de Freitas and 47% for Ciro Gomes; Delegado Huggo, Vera Lúcia and Zé Batista each had 1%. Elmano rose two points and Ciro fell two compared with the previous survey. Their total-vote figures were 46%-44%. Datafolha interviewed 1,560 voters on October 2 and 3, with a two-point margin of error and 95% confidence; O Povo commissioned the survey. Separately, g1 reported that Quaest gave Elmano de Freitas 50% of valid votes and Ciro Gomes 49%.

**Why it matters.** An objection to registration does not establish that the measurement is invalid. Comparison with Quaest must preserve that distinction between formal oversight and electoral results.
