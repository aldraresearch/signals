#### 1. Un proyectil hiere a tripulantes de un petrolero en Ormuz

El Ministerio de Asuntos Exteriores de India confirmó el martes 12 heridos tras el impacto de un proyectil no identificado en el petrolero On Peace, de bandera de Panamá, cuando atravesaba el estrecho de Ormuz. La nueva confirmación identifica un buque petrolero alcanzado durante su tránsito y documenta lesiones entre sus tripulantes.

Al Jazeera citó al mismo ministerio y precisó que al menos 12 de los 19 tripulantes resultaron heridos. Reportes no confirmados en X añaden que Omán evacuó a 10 personas del buque. Las fuentes no establecen quién lanzó el proyectil, un cierre del estrecho ni una variación observada de las primas de seguro. Tampoco aportan un balance de daños estructurales del petrolero.

#### 2. Drones hunden un mercante e incendian otro frente a Bulgaria

El ALFA WATAN, de bandera de Togo, sufrió daños graves y se hundió mientras navegaba desde Turquía hacia Galați, Rumanía. El ferry búlgaro DIOSCURIA encontró una embarcación volcada, tres balsas salvavidas volcadas y chalecos, pero ningún superviviente del ALFA WATAN. Las autoridades vigilan posibles derrames de petróleo y contaminación. Radev calificó el ataque de violación flagrante del derecho internacional y marítimo. Splash247 lo sitúa apenas un día después de la pérdida del Royad Mammadov frente a Rumanía. Las fuentes no establecen autoría.

#### 1. Projectile injures tanker crew during Hormuz transit

India’s Ministry of External Affairs confirmed on Tuesday that 12 crew members were injured when an unidentified projectile struck the Panama-flagged oil tanker On Peace while it was passing through the Strait of Hormuz. The new confirmation identifies a tanker hit during transit and documents injuries among its crew.

The sources do not establish who launched the projectile, a closure of the strait or an observed change in insurance premiums. They also provide no assessment of structural damage to the tanker.

#### 2. Drones sink one merchant ship and ignite another off Bulgaria

Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev confirmed that sea and aerial drones attacked two merchant vessels around 3:00 a.m. inside Bulgaria’s exclusive economic zone, about 70 nautical miles east of Byala. The Togo-flagged ALFA WATAN suffered severe damage and sank while sailing from Turkey toward Galați, Romania. Its crew remained missing and the search continued without survivors located.

The Palau-flagged ABLE was sailing from Romania toward Turkey and caught fire after the strike. Its 18 crew members were rescued; two were injured and are being transported to Varna aboard a Bulgarian Navy vessel. The other 16 are being taken ashore by Border Police. Radev said 11 are Turkish nationals and 7 are Indian nationals.

The Bulgarian ferry DIOSCURIA found an overturned boat, three overturned life rafts and life jackets, but no survivors from ALFA WATAN. Authorities are monitoring for possible oil spills and pollution. Radev called the attack a blatant violation of international and maritime law. Splash247 places it barely a day after the loss of Royad Mammadov off Romania. The sources do not establish responsibility.

Exposure: Logistics, Security · merchant vessel strikes → vessel availability loss → commercial-navigation risk → insurance repricing risk · commercial vessel availability, regional cargo throughput, Black Sea shipping risk, war-risk insurance premiums  
**Aldra assessment** (confidence: moderate, direction: deteriorating). The loss of one vessel and fire aboard another transmit risk to commercial transport availability. They could tighten insurance terms; the sources document neither premium changes nor responsibility for the attack.

**What to watch.** An official Bulgarian maritime notice establishing navigation restrictions in the attacked area.

#### 3. Anduril: Arsenal-2 to make Virginia-class submarine components

The US Department of Defense announced that Donald J. Trump and Pete Hegseth presented the development of Arsenal-2 during a ceremony at the shipyard’s future site in Sparrows Point, Maryland. According to Breaking Defense, Anduril positions the project to manufacture “complex components and large scale assemblies” for Virginia-class submarines. The announcement establishes development of a facility, not production already underway.

Defense News reported that Anduril will invest $3.7 billion in the new shipyard and puts investment by Anduril and the US Navy to boost submarine component production at $6.6 billion. Naval News likewise describes the $3.7 billion as private capital and the additional capacity as prospective. The Financial Times frames the project as part of Trump’s attempt to reduce dependence on leading prime contractors.
