### Main situations

#### 1. Isaias strengthens to 120 mph and approaches Florida Panhandle

Hurricane Isaias intensified overnight, with maximum sustained winds increasing to 120 mph and a better-defined eye. At 1000 AM CDT on October 9, the center was located at 27.7N 87.3W moving NNE at 16 mph with minimum central pressure of 959 mb. It is expected to reach the western Florida Panhandle coast this evening. A Storm Surge Warning is in effect from the Mouth of the Mississippi River to the Suwannee River and a Hurricane Warning from Ocean Springs, MS to the Bay/Gulf County Line, FL. Hurricane Hunter data showed flight-level winds up to 107 kt.

Isaias will likely become a major hurricane soon, but a large increase in shear is anticipated before landfall, which will probably cause some weakening. The forecast calls for high-end category 2 intensity at landfall. It will be a dangerous hurricane with significant storm surge, damaging winds, and flooding rains. The wind field is predicted to grow due to a trough.

#### 2. LPG tanker struck by projectile near United Arab Emirates

According to the UKMTO center, an LPG tanker was struck by unknown projectiles 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera, UAE, at about 10:00 UTC. The strike caused a fire onboard that has since been extinguished. The condition of the crew, the damage and any environmental impact are unknown and authorities are investigating. This follows Wednesday’s strike on a tanker about 51 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar, which reportedly caused casualties.

The location is inside the Persian Gulf, outside the Strait of Hormuz. No route closure or insurance premium changes have been confirmed.
