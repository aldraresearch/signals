### El Ministerio de Minas y Energía de Colombia elimina 15 distritos mineros. El Gobierno colombiano asigna 2,7 billones de pesos al Plan Energía Ya.

### Main situations

#### 1. Colombia: mines ministry abolishes 15 Special Mining Districts

Colombia's Ministry of Mines and Energy abolished the 15 Special Mining Districts. The ministry said the measure restores “legal certainty” to mining regions, but its announcement does not detail which rules will apply after those designations are removed.

According to El Tiempo, the areas covered more than 5.3 million hectares, equivalent to around 5% of national territory, across 11 departments and Colombia's Pacific coast. The official statement confirms the districts' abolition but does not identify the legal instrument implementing it or specify its effects on individual permits or projects.

### Other developments

#### 2. Colombia: government allocates 2.7 trillion pesos to Plan Energía Ya

Facing the risk of a blackout linked to El Niño, Colombia's government set December 31 as the final deadline to implement all preventive measures and allocated 2.7 trillion pesos to Plan Energía Ya, Colombia's Presidency reported. The announcement does not detail those measures: it says they aim to ensure thermal backup, settle power-sector debts and strengthen electricity infrastructure.

Colombia's Ministry of Mines and Energy launched the plan, according to Semana. The Presidency also reported establishing a regulatory framework of tariff incentives to reward electricity savings and launching the “Recursivos por naturaleza” campaign, calling for adaptation and energy efficiency. The statement does not report implementation results.

**Aldra assessment** (confidence: moderate, direction: unclear). The allocation and deadline do not establish an improvement in electricity supply. For generators and users, the effective change depends on funding translating into thermal backup and infrastructure; the announcement does not establish that available generation has increased.

**What to watch.** An official implementation report for Plan Energía Ya detailing thermal backup and debts settled.
