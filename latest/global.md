#### 1. Financial Times: 10-year yields jump half-point

10-year Treasury yields rose half a percentage point in their worst month in four years, according to Financial Times. Bloomberg reported a fresh 24-year high for long-bond yields, with resilient US spending bolstering expectations that the economy can handle higher interest rates.

**Why it matters.** Higher yields make new government borrowing more expensive. Watch whether spending continues to support expectations that the economy can handle higher rates.

#### 2. CENTCOM announces end of Iraq anti-ISIS mission

Sean Parnell and Brad Cooper announced the end of Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq after forces and equipment left Erbil Air Base. According to the announcement, the 12-year mission ends and Iraqi forces take responsibility for remaining ISIS threats. Infobae said the withdrawal was agreed two years ago.

**Why it matters.** The next test is whether Iraqi forces can manage ISIS threats without the coalition mission; the transition changes who is responsible for handling them.
