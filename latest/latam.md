### Main situations

#### 1. Brazil: Estadao reports EWZ down 18.29% after Lula victory

According to Estadao, EWZ, an index fund comprising the largest companies on Brazil's stock exchange and traded in New York, was down 18.29% in overnight trading after Lula's reelection in Brazil on Sunday 25. The outlet also reported sharp increases in dollar and interest-rate futures, describing a correction of bets on a change of government.

Estadao cited a Citi survey of 73 equity and multi-asset fund managers, released Friday: in the event of a Lula victory, 80% expected the Ibovespa to fall more than 5% on Monday 26, while 63% expected the real to depreciate more than 5%. These are respondents' forecasts, not results from that session.

Clarin reported that the dollar rose more than 8% in Brazil after the result became known and that a negative impact on Argentine securities was expected. According to the outlet, Argentina's country risk had closed Friday at 707 points; it provided no new post-election quote.

#### 2. Argentina: energy costs fell 43%, Infobae reports

According to Infobae, Argentina's hydroelectric generation rose almost a third and energy costs fell 43% in a month. The outlet attributes the production increase to record high water on the Paraná in Argentina: greater output from the dams brought wholesale spot electricity prices to their lowest level since April.
